About li4chess

Free four-player chess in your browser

li4chess is a free, ad-free chess game where four armies share one board and compete for the highest final score. Play against computer opponents, take turns with friends on the same device, or watch four computers play. No account, subscription or installation is needed.

Play against computers at five difficulty levels

Choose Play against computers to start as Red against three level-3 opponents. Open Customize game to choose a human or computer for each seat and set each computer's level from 1 to 5. The computers run in your browser, so you can start without waiting for opponents.

How does four-player chess work?

The cross-shaped board has 160 playable squares. Turns go Red → Blue → Yellow → Green, skipping eliminated players. Protect your own king while earning points through captures, checks against multiple kings and checkmates. The highest final points total wins, including points earned by eliminated players; tied scores share a place.

Checkmated and stalemated armies remain on the board as blockers: they cannot move or attack and are worth no capture points. Pawns automatically promote to queens on their eighth rank. The in-game rules and points history explain scoring and game endings in more detail.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, by sharing a device. In Customize game, turn off CPU control for the seats your friends will play, then take turns on the same screen. You can mix human and computer players. Online rooms, matchmaking and accounts are not available on this site.

Can I save a game and come back later?

Games save automatically in this browser when browser storage is available. Choose Resume saved game to continue later on the same browser and device. Starting a new game replaces that save. Use More game tools to export a replay file as a portable backup or import one to restore a game. Saves do not sync between devices.

Play with a mouse, touch or keyboard

Select a piece, then a highlighted destination. Keyboard players can use the arrow keys to navigate, Enter or Space to select and move, and Escape to clear a selection. The board and controls adapt to desktop and phone screens.
